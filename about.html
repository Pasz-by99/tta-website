About Tinahls Triad Agro

Empowering farmers and students through knowledge, innovation and sustainable practices

Our Story

Transforming Agriculture in Namibia

Tinahls Triad Agro CC is an emerging Namibian agribusiness dedicated to transforming the agricultural sector through innovation, education and sustainable farming practices.

We focus on livestock production, crop farming and farming capacity development, with a strong emphasis on empowering both smallholder and commercial farmers. Our goal is to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application.

Based in Ekuku, Oshakati, we serve farmers, students and agripreneurs across all 14 regions of Namibia.

Leadership

Meet the Founder

Albertina Nalukale Mewiliko Shiimi is the Chief Executive Officer and Founder of Tinahls Triad Agro CC.

She brings a strong background in animal production, agribusiness and agricultural training. With experience in project planning, farm management and stakeholder engagement, she provides overall leadership and strategic direction for the company.

Her passion is to equip farmers and students with practical skills that translate into real productivity and profitability on the ground.
