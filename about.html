Transforming Agriculture in Namibia

Tinahls Triad Agro CC is an emerging Namibian agribusiness dedicated to transforming the agricultural sector through innovation, education and sustainable farming practices. We focus on livestock production, crop farming and farming capacity development.

Meet the Founder

Albertina Nalukale Mewiliko Shiimi is the Chief Executive Officer and Founder of Tinahls Triad Agro CC. She brings a strong background in animal production, agribusiness and agricultural training.

With experience in project planning, farm management and stakeholder engagement, she provides overall leadership and strategic direction for the company.

Her passion is to equip farmers and students with practical skills that translate into real productivity and profitability on the ground.

Our Vision

To become a leading agribusiness in Namibia and beyond, recognised for empowering farmers and advancing sustainable agricultural development.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality agricultural training, advisory services and innovative solutions that enhance productivity, profitability and sustainability in livestock and crop production.
