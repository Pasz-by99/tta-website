Hands-on Agricultural Training

We deliver practical and theoretical training designed to improve productivity, profitability and sustainability.

Who We Train

Smallholder and commercial farmers, students, agripreneurs and community groups.

Training Approach

Theory plus practical demonstrations, so you leave with skills to apply on your farm.

Nationwide Reach

Training and outreach programmes available across all 14 regions of Namibia.

Key Training Areas

Livestock Production

Breeding, nutrition, health management and housing for cattle, goats, pigs and poultry.

Crop Production for Feed

Crop selection, planting, soil management and sustainable feed production.

Farm Business & Finance

Record keeping, budgeting, profitability analysis and financial management.

Climate-Smart Farming

Climate-resilient practices that protect the land while improving yields.

Feed Formulation

Feed quality assessment, ration balancing and cost-effective feeding strategies.

Mentorship & Coaching

Ongoing support for young farmers and students entering agriculture.

Interested in a training workshop?

We organise group trainings, on-farm sessions and customised programmes for communities and institutions.
