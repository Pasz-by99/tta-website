Training & Workshops

Practical knowledge that farmers and students can apply immediately

Capacity Building

Hands-on Agricultural Training

We deliver practical and theoretical training designed to improve productivity, profitability and sustainability.

Who We Train

Smallholder farmers, commercial farmers, agricultural students, agripreneurs and community groups across all 14 regions of Namibia.

Training Approach

We combine theory with practical demonstrations so participants leave with skills they can implement on their own farms.

Nationwide Reach

Our training and outreach programmes are available across all 14 regions of Namibia.

Key Training Areas

Livestock Production

Breeding, nutrition, health management, housing systems for cattle, goats, pigs and poultry.

Crop Production for Feed

Crop selection, planting techniques, soil management and sustainable practices for animal feed production.

Farm Business & Finance

Record keeping, enterprise budgeting, profitability analysis and farm financial management.

Climate-Smart Farming

Sustainable and climate-resilient practices that protect the land while improving yields.

Feed Formulation

Practical feed quality assessment, ration balancing and cost-effective feeding strategies.

Mentorship & Coaching

Ongoing support for young farmers and students entering the agricultural sector.

Interested in a training workshop?

We organise group trainings, on-farm sessions and customised programmes for communities and institutions.
