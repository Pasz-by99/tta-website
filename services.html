Complete Agricultural Support Services

We provide practical, knowledge-based solutions designed to improve farm performance across Namibia.

01. Training & Capacity Building

  • Crop & livestock production training
  • Farm business & financial management
  • Sustainable & climate-smart practices
  • Farmer mentorship & coaching

02. Farm Management Consultancy

  • Farm management planning
  • Profitability assessments
  • Resource optimisation
  • Enterprise budgeting
  • Monitoring & evaluation

03. Management Calendars & Planning

  • Custom crop production calendars
  • Livestock management calendars
  • Seasonal operation schedules
  • Breeding, vaccination & health calendars
  • Grazing & pasture management plans

  • Farm record-keeping systems
  • Livestock & breeding records
  • Financial & inventory records
  • Crop production & yield records
  • Data collection, analysis & reporting

05. Livestock & Crop Production Services

  • Breeding & selection advisory
  • Herd & flock management
  • Animal nutrition & feeding programs
  • Feed & pasture development
  • Livestock housing & welfare
  • Crop production & forage establishment

06. Feed & Nutrition Analysis

  • Feed quality assessment
  • Nutritional composition analysis
  • Feed formulation & ration balancing
  • Feed budgeting & feed security planning
  • Diet-based management calendars

07. Input Supply & Farm Materials

  • Chicks (day-old & growers)
  • Piglets (breeding & growers)
  • Seeds (crops, vegetables, legumes)
  • Seedlings (fruit trees & vegetables)
  • Animal feed & feed ingredients
  • Herbs, medicinal plants & tools
